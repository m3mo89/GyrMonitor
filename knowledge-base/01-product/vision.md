# Product Direction

## Direction Statement

GyrMonitor MVP is a software platform for monitoring cattle inactivity through structured activity events, alerts, observations and offline synchronization.

The product focuses on validating the complete operational workflow before adding any advanced automated data source.

## Product Intent

The MVP must help users:

- Register and consult cattle records.
- Capture activity and inactivity events.
- Identify animals with prolonged inactivity.
- Generate alerts based on risk indicators.
- Record field observations.
- Work with intermittent connectivity.
- Consult dashboard metrics and historical information.

## MVP Boundary

The MVP only consumes structured data. It does not produce events from automated detection pipelines or specialized hardware.

## Long-Term Direction

Future versions may add new event-generation mechanisms, but the MVP must remain focused on the documented software platform.
