## Product Summary

GyrMonitor supports early identification of prolonged inactivity in Gyr cattle by turning activity events into operational alerts, observations, and dashboard indicators.

The product is designed for rural contexts where internet connectivity may be intermittent and where field staff need to continue working even when offline.
